    Roadmap to the Platinum

      Save rules that protect the whole run

      • No mission replay and no New Game Plus: keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter.
      • One trophy may be missable — Positive Mental Attitude. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1.
      • Cheats disable trophies and saving on your trophy save. Keep them off.
      • Everything except that one meditation stays available in free roam after the credits.
