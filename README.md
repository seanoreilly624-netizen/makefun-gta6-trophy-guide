[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-trophy-guide-01/seanoreilly624-netizen/makefun-gta6-trophy-guide/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01)

An offline web app that tracks all 55 GTA 6 PlayStation 5 trophies on the road to the Platinum.

Open `app/index.html` in a browser: check trophies off as you earn them, filter by story, missable, collectibles or activities, search, and follow the 8-stage roadmap (~200 hours). Progress saves in the browser. Every trophy guide cites its sources.

Built for the [make.fun GTA 6 trophy guide bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-trophy-guide-01).
